Chateau La Tour Blanche, or La Tour-Branche is a sweet white wine ranked as First Growth in the Original Bordeaux Wine Official Classification of 1855.

*First Growth - highest and most prestigious tier a winery can get.*

### History

* Estate origins to 18th century, connect them to **Jean Saint Marc du Latorublanche**, treasurer general to Louis XIV.
* Post French Revolution, it was owned by **Pierre Pecherie**.
* Later it was the German owner **Frederic Focke** who was credited with bringing the idea of \`noble rot\` which helped the winery secure it's First Growth Ranking.
* Later, it was acquired by **Daniel Ifla (aka Osiris).**
* He didn't have any heirs, so after he died, he passed it over to French state on a condition to open a winery school on the property.
* Since then, it has been owned by the **French state**

### Birthplace of Modern Sauternes

* Heavily credited with inventing how Sauternes wine is made today.

### Rare Grape Blend

* While most Sauternes vineyards rely strictly on Semillon and Sauvignon Blanc, La Tour Blanche maints a notably **high percentage of Muscadelle**. 
* This rare grape injects highly distinct exotic, and spicy complexities into their sweet wines.
